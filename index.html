The plan

Everything is in scope; it arrives in three phases. Revenue and customer support come first. Open a main point to see its parts, then open a part to see exactly what it includes.

RevenueBrings in or protects sales and margin. Top priority.
Customer supportAnswers customers faster and keeps them coming back. Top priority.
OperationsRuns the shop, people and money behind the scenes.
FoundationLogins, data, backups and safety nets everything else needs.

Mockup only. Nothing is connected, and all names and numbers on the dashboard are samples.
